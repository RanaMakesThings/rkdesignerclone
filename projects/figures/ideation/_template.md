## Candidate Directions

List 6 to 12 synthesized options. These should come from:

- your own Codex pass
- the model artifact pass
- merged judgment after removing duplicates and weak ideas

For each option include:

- `name`
- `composition`
- `placement`
- `assetMode`: `native | photo | hybrid`
- `imageQueries`: empty for `native`; 1 to 3 concrete Pexels queries for
  `photo` or `hybrid`
- `why it works`
- `main risk`

## Shortlist

- 2 to 4 directions worth building
- For each shortlisted `photo` or `hybrid` direction, include:
  - image-board path
  - keep / discard verdict
  - best candidate note
