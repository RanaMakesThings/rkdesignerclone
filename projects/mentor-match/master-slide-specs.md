## Deck spine

1. Mentorship is valuable, but access to the right mentor is still too
   dependent on chance, visibility, and manual coordination.
2. Mentor Match turns mentor discovery and pairing into a lightweight workflow
   with clearer inputs, better fit, and easier first contact.
3. The first milestone is a narrow pilot that proves demand, match quality,
   and operational repeatability before the concept scales.
