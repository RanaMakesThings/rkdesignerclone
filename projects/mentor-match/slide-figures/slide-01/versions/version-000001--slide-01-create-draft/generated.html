What this talk will cover
The problem, the product, and the pilot goals.
A short overview of why matching is hard today, how Mentor Match simplifies it, and what an early pilot needs to prove.
1
The problem
People want guidance, but finding the right mentor is often slow, ambiguous, and inconsistent.
2
The product
Mentor Match narrows choices into a clearer, better-fit connection with shared goals and practical next steps.
3
The pilot goals
We will test whether stronger matching improves confidence, response quality, and follow-through in early use.
