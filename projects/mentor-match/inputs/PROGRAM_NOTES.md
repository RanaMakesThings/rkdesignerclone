# Program Notes

Working notes for the Mentor Match overview deck.

## Current state

- This is a first-pass narrative scaffold.
- No imported Mentor Match source deck, brief, or product requirements doc was
  found in the workspace when this project was created.
- The talk target is seven minutes.

## Working product assumption

Assume Mentor Match is a product or program that helps people find and start
the right mentoring relationship through a more structured matching workflow.

That assumption currently includes:

- mentees share goals, context, or preferences
- mentors surface relevant experience, availability, or interests
- the system or process supports fit-based matching
- the first introduction is guided so the relationship starts with context

## Working audience assumption

Assume the audience is a mixed stakeholder group that needs a clear summary of:

- what Mentor Match is
- why it matters
- what the first phase is trying to prove

## Working deck goal

Use this deck to explain:

- the current problem with ad hoc mentor discovery
- the Mentor Match solution at a glance
- how the matching flow works
- the value proposition and pilot goals

## Open questions to replace later

- Who is the primary audience for this talk?
- Is Mentor Match already live, piloting, or still conceptual?
- What exact inputs determine a good match?
- Is there a specific institution, company, or community context?

## Guardrails

- Do not claim proven retention, equity, or career outcomes without source
  evidence.
- Do not imply the exact matching logic is settled until product details are
  confirmed.
- Keep value language directional until pilot results exist.
