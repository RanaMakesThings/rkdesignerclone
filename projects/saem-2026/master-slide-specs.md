## Deck spine

1. Emergency medicine has a learning-design problem, not a learner-motivation problem.
2. Microlearning works because of learning science, not because it is short.
3. FOAMed, mobile platforms, and AI-enabled tools make delivery and adaptation more practical.
4. Workflow fit, curation, and governance are the real implementation constraints.
5. Start with one small pilot and measure honestly.
