## Locked slide thesis

Good microlearning is brief, specific, active, and built for low-friction use.

## Slide purpose brief

- top claim:
  good microlearning is intentionally constrained
- support claim:
  relevance, activation, and ease of use matter more than production gloss
- non-goals:
  - do not become a bullet wall
  - do not look like a product feature list

## Figure job

## Locked copy

- header:
  `Design rules for good microlearning.`
- subheader:
  `Keep it brief, specific, active, and mobile-first.`
- figure labels:
  - `one objective`
    `one decision or skill per unit`
  - `clinical relevance`
    `anchor every unit to a patient problem`
  - `active response`
    `prompt retrieval, not passive recognition`
  - `mobile friction`
    `zero login, zero setup, zero pinch-zoom`
  - `feedback`
    `show why the answer works`
