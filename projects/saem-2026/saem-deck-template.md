# SAEM Deck Template Contract

## Source

Use the official SAEM annual meeting PowerPoint template stored at:

`projects/saem-2026/templates/saem-annual-meeting-template-v1/saem_annual_meeting_template.pptx`

## Rule

Designer is the exploration surface.

The SAEM template is the final packaging surface.

That means:

- first drafts should optimize for clarity, hierarchy, and visual quality
- final export should respect SAEM branding and conference constraints

## Visual constraints

- serious academic conference tone
- readable in a large room
- no generic corporate bullet walls
- no startup-style hype language
- no cartoonish med-tech visuals
- preserve space for citations or evidence anchors when needed

## Template debt policy

If the official SAEM template feels visually plain, do not let that flatten the
designer exploration phase. Instead:

- design the strongest version first
- then adapt it into the SAEM shell with minimal quality loss
