# Visual Source Doc

## General direction

This deck should feel:

- serious
- editorial
- calm under pressure
- modern without looking trendy
- appropriate for a national academic medical meeting

## Avoid

- generic corporate templates
- dense bullet walls
- cheesy medical stock imagery
- startup hype visuals
- purple-heavy palettes
- gimmicky gamification motifs

## Preferred design language

- strong hierarchy
- clean 16:9 composition
- generous margins
- restrained accent color
- diagrams over decoration
- abstract or editorial metaphor over literal clip art

## Deck-level visual arc

- opening:
  signal through noise
- middle:
  frameworks, ecosystems, workflow maps
- closing:
  practical confidence and visual resolution

## Template relationship

The official SAEM annual meeting template is real and should be used for final
delivery, but early designer concepts should not be trapped by the plainest
possible interpretation of that PowerPoint shell.
