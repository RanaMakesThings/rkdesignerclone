# Copy Source Doc

## Audience

- emergency medicine educators
- emergency physicians
- education leaders at SAEM

## Talk goal

Make the case that microlearning is rigorous, practical, and especially well
suited to emergency medicine when grounded in learning science and paired with
curation, workflow fit, and thoughtful use of newer tools such as AI.

## Tone

- evidence-based
- modern
- pragmatic
- not evangelistic
- not anti-FOAM
- not breathless about AI

## Core thesis

Microlearning is not "smaller teaching." In emergency medicine, it is a
workflow-aligned design strategy grounded in learning science that can improve
retention, usability, and uptake when paired with good curation and thoughtful
evaluation.

## Claim guardrails

- Do not imply that all FOAMed is high quality.
- Do not overclaim patient-outcome evidence.
- Do not imply that short content is inherently effective.
- Frame AI and XR as emerging, not established standard practice.
- Keep citations present where they add credibility, but quiet enough that they do not dominate the slide.
