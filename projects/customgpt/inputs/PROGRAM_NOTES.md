# Program Notes

## Session

- Working title:
  `CustomGPT Creation`
- Venue:
  SAEM annual meeting
- Format:
  workshop / didactic with live build segment
- Desired structure:
  first 20 minutes on orientation and concept framing, second 20 minutes on
  live walkthrough and testing

## Presenters

- Connor Grant, MD
- Rana Kabeer, MD
- Eddie Garcia, MD

## Audience

- emergency medicine residency leaders
- chief residents
- coordinators or administrators
- faculty with mixed technical comfort levels

## Working assumptions

- the audience range includes tech-illiterate attendees and some tech-savvy
  attendees
- the current deck is too theory-heavy up front
- the workshop should focus less on generic transformer explanations and more
  on practical product understanding and demo literacy

## Speaker goals

- explain what the assistant is in plain language
- show why it can be useful for residency administration
- build one live in the current ChatGPT product surface
- show what good and bad answers look like
- leave the audience with safe governance rules
