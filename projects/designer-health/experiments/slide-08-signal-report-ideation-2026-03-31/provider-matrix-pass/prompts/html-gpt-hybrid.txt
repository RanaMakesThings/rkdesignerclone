Build one investor-deck figure for Designer Health slide 8.

This figure should be one central clinician-facing report sheet on a quiet
deck background. It should make the viewer instantly understand that the system
turns messy patient conversation into a structured brief with evidence,
considerations, and the next question.

Use the attached screenshot as family reference:
- preserve the row anatomy on the left
- preserve the ranked reasoning blocks on the right
- preserve the calm thin-border utility aesthetic
- do not keep the screenshot as two equal cards

Locked direction:
- one shared outer sheet
- left column wider than right
- right side is a reasoning rail, not a co-equal workspace
- full-width bottom timeline strip reunifies the whole artifact

Hard constraints:
- no browser chrome
- no fake EHR nav
- no trace tabs
- no giant diagnosis headline
- no busy chip soup

Layout proportions:
- left column around 62% to 66%
- right rail around 34% to 38%
- bottom timeline strip across full width

Top header:
- artifact title such as `Structured Brief` or `Intake Brief`
- quiet subtitle / case descriptor
- should feel editorial and clinical, not marketing

Left column content:
- section groups like:
  - SOCIAL CONTEXT
  - SYMPTOMS
  - KEY NEGATIVES
  - WORKUP / UNCERTAINTIES
- rows should retain:
  - label
  - optional value line
  - evidence line
  - state pill
- rows can remain lightly bordered or ledger-like, but must be cleaner than the
  source screenshot

Left state-pill semantics:
- present = strongest neutral pill
- absent = quieter gray pill
- unresolved = warm gray or pale amber pill
- do not use green for present

Right rail content:
- title such as `Clinical Considerations` or `Working Considerations`
- three ranked candidate blocks
- each block includes:
  - rank
  - diagnosis title
  - confidence
  - compact movement chip only if it is clean enough, for example `↑ 2→1`,
    `— 1→1`, `↓ 1→3`
  - Supports
  - Against
  - To confirm / Unknowns
- glyphs on the right can use very small typographic marks:
  - `↑` muted green
  - `↓` muted red
  - `○` or `?` amber

Bottom timeline strip:
- must run full width
- should visually reunify the sheet
- include a simple chronological sequence:
  - URI symptoms begin
  - cough develops about one week ago
  - yellow sputum noted
  - fever / breathing impact still unresolved

Use real cough-case content:
- Care seeking prompted by spouse
- Cough
- Cough pattern / frequency
- Worse at night
- Worse when lying down
- Scratchy throat
- Sputum color mostly yellow
- Hemoptysis absent
- Dyspnea / breathing impact
- Fever unresolved or not yet confirmed

Use three considerations:
- Community-acquired pneumonia
- Acute bronchitis
- Post-viral cough

Add one explicit handoff block near the right or above the timeline:
- `Suggested next question`
- “How has your breathing changed, and does it hurt anywhere in the chest when
  you take a deep breath?”

The result should feel like:
- the best hybrid of the source screenshot and a deck-safe clinical document
- a single tangible artifact
- strong enough to screenshot and drop directly into the slide
