#1
Community-acquired pneumonia
medium
↑ 2→1
Supports
  • Persistent cough with possible breathing impact
  • Yellow sputum raises possibility of lower respiratory infection
  • Nighttime symptoms can coexist with infectious process
Against
  • No hemoptysis reported
  • No confirmed fever or systemic symptoms yet documented
  • No objective vitals, oxygen data, or exam findings
To confirm / unknowns
  • Pleuritic chest pain, exertional dyspnea, rigors
  • Temperature, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation
  • Focal lung findings or imaging if indicated
#2
Acute bronchitis
high
— 1→2
Supports
  • Scratchy throat / URI-like prodrome before cough
  • Cough occurs in bursts with intermittent productive sputum
  • Yellow sputum can occur in viral or post-infectious bronchitis
Against
  • Meaningful breathing limitation would require reassessment for alternatives
  • Positional worsening may suggest overlapping causes or incomplete history
To confirm / unknowns
  • Severity and progression of dyspnea
  • Any fever, fatigue, or reduced activity tolerance
  • Exam findings to exclude focal process
