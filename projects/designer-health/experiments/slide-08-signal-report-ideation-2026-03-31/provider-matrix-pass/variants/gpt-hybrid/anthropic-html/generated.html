Working Considerations
#1 Acute bronchitis
high ↑ 2→1
Supports
  • URI-like prodrome preceding cough by a few days
  • Course ~1 week, cough in bursts/fits
  • Intermittent productive sputum, yellow
  • Hemoptysis absent
Against
  • Nocturnal/positional worsening could suggest non-bronchitis contributors
To confirm
  • Fever/chills, fatigue severity
  • Vitals (RR, O₂ sat) and lung exam
#2 Community-acquired pneumonia
medium ↓ 1→2
Supports
  • Persistent productive cough with exertional dyspnea
  • Yellow sputum; possible green once
Against
  • No hemoptysis reported
  • No documented fever or systemic symptoms
  • No objective data yet (vitals, imaging)
To confirm
  • O₂ sat, respiratory rate, temperature
  • Pleuritic chest pain, rigors
  • Chest imaging
#3 Post-viral cough
low ○ 3→3
Supports
  • URI prodrome consistent with viral trigger
  • Nocturnal/positional cough pattern
Against
  • Only ~1 week; typically diagnosed when cough persists >3 weeks
  • Ongoing productive sputum less typical
To confirm
  • Duration trajectory over coming weeks
Suggested Next Question
"How has your breathing changed—do you get short of breath walking across a room? And does it hurt anywhere in your chest when you take a deep breath?"
