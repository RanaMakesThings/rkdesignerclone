Working Considerations
#1 Acute bronchitis (viral or post-infectious cough)
high stable
Supports
  • URI-like prodrome: scratchy throat preceded cough by a few days
  • Overall course approximately one week
  • Cough in bursts/fits with periods of relative calm
  • Intermittent productive sputum (esp. mornings); yellow sputum can occur
Against
  • Exertional dyspnea needs alternative causes (e.g., pneumonia) excluded
  • Nocturnal/positional worsening could suggest non-bronchitis contributors
Unknowns
  • Fever/chills, fatigue severity
  • Vitals (RR, O₂ sat) and lung exam findings
  • Dyspnea severity/progression and ability to speak/walk
#2 Community-acquired pneumonia
medium stable
Supports
  • Persistent cough with exertional dyspnea
  • Productive sputum (yellow; possible green once, uncertain)
Against
  • No hemoptysis reported
  • No documented fever or systemic symptoms
  • No objective data (vitals, O₂ sat, lung exam, imaging)
Unknowns
  • Oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, temperature
  • Pleuritic chest pain, rigors, worsening dyspnea
  • Focal lung findings or chest imaging
#3 Post-nasal drip / upper airway cough syndrome
low stable
Supports
  • Nocturnal and positional worsening consistent with post-nasal drip
  • Scratchy throat may reflect pharyngeal irritation from drainage
Against
  • No reported nasal congestion, rhinorrhea, or sinus pressure
  • Sputum character (yellow) more typical of lower airway process
Unknowns
  • Allergy history, nasal exam findings
  • Throat-clearing habit, sensation of drainage
