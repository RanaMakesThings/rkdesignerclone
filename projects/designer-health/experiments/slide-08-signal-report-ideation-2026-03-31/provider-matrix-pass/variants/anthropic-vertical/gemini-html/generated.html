Working Considerations

#1 Acute bronchitis (viral or post-infectious)

High Stable

Supports

  • URI-like prodrome (scratchy throat)
  • Overall course ~1 week
  • Cough in bursts/fits with relative calm
  • Intermittent productive sputum (yellow)

Against

  • Exertional dyspnea needs alternative causes excluded
  • Nocturnal/positional worsening could suggest non-bronchitis contributors

Unknowns

  • Fever/chills severity
  • Vitals (RR, O2 sat) and lung exam
  • True dyspnea severity/progression

#2 Community-acquired pneumonia

Medium Stable

Supports

  • Persistent cough with potential exertional dyspnea
  • Productive sputum (yellow/green)

Against

  • No hemoptysis reported
  • No documented fever or severe systemic symptoms
  • No objective data yet

Unknowns

  • Oxygen saturation, respiratory rate
  • Pleuritic chest pain, rigors
  • Focal lung findings or chest imaging

#3 Post-viral cough

Low Stable

Supports

  • Scratchy throat preceded cough
  • Cough worse at night / lying down

Against

  • Productive sputum is less typical (usually dry)
  • Timeline may be too short (~1 week)

Unknowns

  • Exact timeline of initial URI resolution
  • Presence of post-nasal drip

Suggested Clinician Handoff Questions

How has your breathing changed, especially when walking or climbing stairs?

Do you experience any sharp chest pain when taking a deep breath?

Is the feeling of being warm ongoing, or has it resolved?
