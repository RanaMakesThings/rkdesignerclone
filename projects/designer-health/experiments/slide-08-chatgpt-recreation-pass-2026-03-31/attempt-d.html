HPI & context

Need SpO2 Need respiratory rate

Working assessment

1. Acute bronchitis / viral post-infectious cough

Best fits the five-day timeline and prodrome, but dyspnea and fever warrant ruling out overlap or secondary complication.

2. Community-acquired pneumonia

Fever, productive cough, and dyspnea keep this live until objective respiratory findings are clearer.

Interview strategy
  • Clarify limitation: “How far can you walk right now compared with last week?”
  • Establish baseline: “Have you ever been diagnosed with asthma or COPD?”
  • Trajectory: “Do you feel like you are getting better, or is the breathing getting worse?”
