# Slide 12 - The Ask

Locked on April 6, 2026.

## Locked headline

Raising $1.25M to launch pilots, prove value, and establish repeatable deployment

## Slide job

Close the deck with a concrete financing ask that feels like a de-risking
plan, not a generic raise.

## Core claim

We are raising $1.25M to fund the next proof stage: launch pilots,
demonstrate value, and show deployment can be repeated.

## What the audience should believe after this slide

This round is sized for a disciplined next step. It funds the exact work
needed to turn the product story into pilot proof and seed readiness.

## Strategic role in deck

This is the closing conversion slide. It should translate the entire deck
from thesis into financing logic.

## Figure plan

The slide should be visually simple and read in three zones.

### Left hero block

Large raise amount and structure:

- $1.25M
- Pre-Seed SAFE
- 18-20 months runway

### Right milestone area

Three clean cards:

- Build
- Deploy
- Validate

### Bottom ribbon

One restrained use-of-funds line, not a budget table:

- product + engineering
- pilot implementation + ops
- infra, security, compliance, measurement

## Why this version is better

It is clearer and faster than the old trust / capture / repeatable deployment
wording. "Prove value" is broader, cleaner, and more investor-legible on a
slide.

## What to avoid

- a dense use-of-funds table
- salary-heavy budget detail
- fuzzy wording like "clinician trust" on the headline
- too many milestones
- a generic close like "build team / go to market / scale"
