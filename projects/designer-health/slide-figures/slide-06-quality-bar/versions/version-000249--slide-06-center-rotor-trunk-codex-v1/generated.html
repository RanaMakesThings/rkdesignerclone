Vox is the adaptive interviewing system that takes on the hard clinical reasoning work needed to turn patient story into a clinician-grade starting point.

Vox is an adaptive clinical interviewer built to produce a clinician-grade starting point.
By combining adaptive follow-up, clinical reasoning, and medical knowledge, Vox identifies what matters, organizes what connects, and surfaces what still needs verification before the visit begins.

My back has been hurting...
Vox: What makes it worse?
It’s worse in the morning.
Vox: When did this start?
Started last Tuesday.
