Panel cards — locked component
Large positioning containers. Three variants: accent (teal banner, "this is us"), neutral (gray banner, "this is them"), and open (no banner, heading inline). The inset graphic is optional and sits flush to the bottom-right corner with a radial fade from the variant's tint color to transparent.
