## Why this slide exists

- define the category gap without letting Vox collapse into scribe-adjacent AI
- show that AI buy-in is already real in clinical documentation
- show that the encounter itself is still comparatively under-served
- make Vox feel like the company addressing the thinner, more central territory

## Locked slide thesis

AI already has real clinician buy-in after the visit, especially in
documentation. The encounter itself is still thinly served. Vox applies AI to
the visit itself.

## Slide purpose brief

- top claim:
  AI adoption is already validated after the visit
- support claim:
  that adoption is clustering around documentation and clerical relief, not the
  encounter itself
- strategic claim:
  Vox is attacking the thinner category inside the live encounter
- non-goals:
  - do not make this an anti-scribe slide
  - do not turn it into a consulting competitor matrix
  - do not drift back into workflow-explainer strips
  - do not rely on medium-sized illustrations or polite caption cards to carry
    the argument

## Locked copy

- headline:
  `AI has traction after the visit. The visit itself is still thinly served.`
- left kicker:
  `AI impact after the visit`
- left support line:
  `Ambient scribes validate clinician buy-in for AI assistance.`
- right kicker:
  `AI impact during the visit`
- right support line:
  `Few tools actually change how the encounter is conducted. Vox does.`

## Draft 1 — Market field map

### Draft 1 concept

- mostly typography and simple shapes
- no illustration-led composition
- two territories under one headline:
  - `After the visit`
  - `During the visit`
- the argument should be visible in density itself:
  left occupied, right open

### Draft 1 required signals

- the after-visit zone should feel denser, darker, and already occupied
- the after-visit cluster should include:
  - `ambient scribe`
  - `note generation`
  - `documentation support`
  - `clerical relief`
- the during-visit zone should feel larger, calmer, and visibly thinner
- the during-visit zone should contain:
  - one blue `VOX` marker
  - optionally one or two faint ghost markers
- the composition should feel editorial, not card-based

### Draft 1 avoid

- illustrations
- photo vignettes
- chip rows doing the real work
- rounded white comparison cards
- heavy grids or matrix chrome
