## Locked slide thesis

History is the cleanest place to reclaim minutes because valuable visit time is
spent turning patient story into decision-ready structure, and that work is
repeated, time-variable, and able to begin before the encounter.

## Slide purpose brief

- top claim:
  history is the best wedge to pull earlier
- supporting claim:
  this wedge is where patient story gets turned into usable structure
- non-goals:
  - do not show a literal pre-visit zone
  - do not show an encounter boundary
  - do not show a workflow strip
  - do not show fake product UI

## Figure job
