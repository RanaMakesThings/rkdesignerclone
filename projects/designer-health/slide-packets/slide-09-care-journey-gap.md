# Slide 9 — Starting Point Positioning

## Status

- current deck numbering: slide 9
- legacy source mapping: old slide 9
- decision state:
  `starting-point strip` selected and promoted as the current official pass

## Why this slide exists

- define the category gap without drifting into a market map
- show that adjacent workflow support exists, but the clinician still begins
  the encounter without enough structure
- make it explicit that Vox is the layer that changes the clinician's starting
  state
- bridge the product-proof slides into the category and economics slides

## Locked slide thesis

Current assistive AI mostly helps before, during, or after the encounter. Vox
changes how the visit starts by giving the clinician a structured brief at the
moment clinical reasoning begins.

## Slide purpose brief

- top claim:
  assistive tools exist around the visit, but Vox is the layer that changes the
  clinician's starting point
- support claim:
  the difference should be located spatially in the care journey, not argued as
  a competitor matrix
- non-goals:
  - do not make this an anti-scribe slide
  - do not turn it into a two-column category comparison unless the journey-map
    family fails completely
  - do not overload the slide with commentary or vendor taxonomy

## Figure job

- map the care journey from before the visit through during and after
- make the `Start of visit` zone the obvious hero
- show adjacent workflow support as real but quieter context
- place one explicit `Vox-prepared brief` object at the start-of-visit point
- make it obvious that Vox changes the starting state before decisions begin

## Visual rules

- white background
- grayscale base
- one restrained blue accent
- one horizontal journey or threshold object across the slide
- supported zones feel validated but quiet
- `Start of visit` should be accent-emphasized
- the center `Vox-prepared brief` should be the primary blue object
- the slide should read in one sweep from left to right

## Reference logic

- this is the first category-gap slide after the more product-tangible slides,
  so it should stay crisp, not argumentative
- keep the deck's premium grammar: whitespace, rounded geometry, minimal chrome
- preserve credibility by treating the supported zones as real context rather
  than straw men
- quote the slide-8 output state conceptually by making the center brief feel
  like a structured clinical object rather than a generic placeholder
