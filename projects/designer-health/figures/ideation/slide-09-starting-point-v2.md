## Codex read on the figure problem

- the old framing made the middle of the slide feel like an abstract gap
- the corrected framing is that Vox is the layer in the middle
- the figure has to show both truths at once:
  - support already exists around the visit
  - Vox is what changes the clinician's starting state
- the center object should quote the slide-8 end state enough to feel like a
  real structured brief

## Four directions to draft

- `Journey Strip + Vox Brief`
  - clearest native direction
  - one horizontal workflow with gray outer zones and a blue center brief
- `Encounter Threshold + Vox Brief`
  - stronger strategic framing
  - the center brief sits on the threshold where the visit starts
- `Hallway Context + Vox Brief`
  - strongest photo branch
  - the environment gives clinical context without showing people
- `Clipboard Context + Vox Brief`
  - strongest hybrid branch
  - the paper surface makes the missing starting structure tangible

## Why these four are the right set

- they all solve the core conceptual problem:
  Vox must be explicit in the middle
- they span two native abstractions plus one photo and one hybrid branch
- they are easier to compare than the earlier more symbolic node/timeline
  options
