## Synthesis

- keep the first canonical pass native
- use one rounded care-journey strip with three zones:
  `Before visit`, `Start of visit`, `During / after`
- let the middle zone stay open with a restrained accent outline and very sparse
  unmet-need cues
- keep the supported zones quiet, with only a few small pills so the slide does
  not turn into a capability cloud
- hold the photo and hybrid branches as challenge passes only if the native
  composition feels too sterile in review

## Chosen starting direction

### Clean Rounded Journey Strip

- one horizontal hero strip across the slide
- muted filled zones on the left and right with small support tags
- open middle zone with accent outline and 2 to 3 compact unmet-need cues
- minimal timeline logic only if it sharpens left-to-right reading
- no icons unless the first native composition truly needs one anchor signal
