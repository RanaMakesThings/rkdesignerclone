# Slide 10 Callback Ideation — Capacity Motif Options v1

## Context

Slide `10` currently works as an economics proof slide.

The new idea is to explicitly callback to slide `3`'s lower compounding motif:

- shortened repeated visit rows
- final green `New visit` payoff block

That callback could make the economics claim feel less abstract by showing that
the added visit is the same capacity object already introduced earlier in the
deck.

## Goal

Make it unmistakable that:

- efficiency repeated across visits creates capacity
- that capacity shows up as one added visit
- that added visit produces the economics on this slide

The callback should strengthen the slide, not turn it into a replay of slide `3`.

## Option 1 — Left Capacity Motif + Right Equation

Layout:

- header/subheader remain across the top
- left side becomes a compact slide-3 callback object:
  - two shortened visit mini-bars
  - one green `New visit` block
  - maybe a tiny label like `Added capacity`
- right side remains the equation/economics proof:
  - `+1 visit / provider / day`
  - assumptions
  - `$11,000 / year`
  - break-even badge

Why it works:

- best explicit linkage between earlier capacity motif and current dollars
- easy left-to-right read: capacity object -> economics consequence
- makes the `+1 visit` less abstract

Main risk:

- can feel too split-screen if the left callback object is too large
- if overbuilt, it becomes a repeat of slide `3`

## Option 2 — Callback Embedded Inside the Hero Card

Layout:

- keep current slide-10 structure mostly intact
- inside the white hero card, place a compact callback strip on the far left:
  - one shortened mini-visit row plus green `New visit`
- then bridge directly into the economics math

Why it works:

- preserves the current clean slide
- ties the callback directly into the economics chain
- keeps one dominant object instead of a two-panel slide

Main risk:

- callback may become too tiny to read
- could feel clever but not materially more persuasive

## Option 3 — Bottom Capacity Strip Replaces the Quiet Range Rail

Layout:

- keep the current equation hero largely intact
- replace the tiny bottom range/check strip with a compact slide-3 callback strip:
  - repeated shortened visits
  - green `New visit`
  - maybe a caption like `One added visit / provider / day`

Why it works:

- strongest continuity with slide `3`
- low disruption to the current selected slide
- keeps the economics hero dominant

Main risk:

- may be too subtle
- the callback can read as decorative rather than causal

## Working judgment before model review

Current ranking:

1. `Option 1 — Left Capacity Motif + Right Equation`
2. `Option 2 — Callback Embedded Inside the Hero Card`
3. `Option 3 — Bottom Capacity Strip Replaces the Quiet Range Rail`

Reason:

The callback should probably do real narrative work, not just decorate the
existing slide. Option 1 gives it the clearest causal role without forcing a
full table or a new finance metaphor.
