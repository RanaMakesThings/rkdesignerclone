## Direction

### Beachhead Profile Hero + Proof Scorecard

- make the beachhead definition feel like one chosen operating profile
- use one dominant hero object:
  - `High-throughput groups with simple deployment`
- attach three qualifying conditions:
  - `visible visit pressure`
  - `simple buying`
  - `low deployment friction`
- keep example settings visibly secondary
- place one compact proof scorecard on the right

## Why this is promising

- it makes the operating profile the thesis
- it avoids naming one specialty as the wedge
- it gives the investor a clear answer to both:
  - where to start
  - what the first pilot must prove

## Design rules

- no funnel grammar
- no filter stacks
- no explicit market scoring grid
- profile card must dominate
- example settings must read as examples, not options to compare
- proof scorecard must stay compact and operational
