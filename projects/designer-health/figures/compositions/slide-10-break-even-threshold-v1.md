## Direction

### Break-even Threshold

- lead with the buyer-relevant threshold:
  - `~$917 / month break-even spend / provider`
- support it with:
  - `+1 visit / provider / day`
  - `$11,000 / year`
- assumptions can live as a quiet row beneath
- use a tiny low/base/high check only if it materially helps

## Why this is promising

- directly frames what the economics unlock means in spend terms
- could set up pricing logic well later

## Design rules

- threshold number dominates first
- annual contribution stays clearly visible
- do not lose the title logic of real capacity
- do not make this feel like a pricing slide
