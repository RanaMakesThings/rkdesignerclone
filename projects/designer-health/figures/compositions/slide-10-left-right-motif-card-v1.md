# Slide 10 Composition Study

- `slug`: `slide-10-left-right-motif-card-v1`
- `slide`: `10`
- `deck`: `designer-health`
- `status`: `ready-for-gemini`

## Direction

### Motif + Economics Card

- left side:
  - compact callback to slide `3`
  - exactly two shortened visit bars plus one green `New visit` block
  - no ruler, no bracket, no full before/after replay
- right side:
  - simple economics proof
  - `+1 visit / provider / day`
  - assumptions
  - `$11,000 / year`
  - optional very quiet upside note for `+2`

## Why this is promising

- strongest overlap across GPT, Gemini, and Claude
- preserves the slide-3 callback without replaying slide `3`
- makes the causal read very direct:
  callback motif -> extra visit -> dollars

## Guardrails

- left motif must stay recognizably derived from slide `3`
- left motif must not become a mini chart
- right side must remain visually dominant
