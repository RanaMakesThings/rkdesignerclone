## Composition options considered

### Selected Native Direction

- use the exact slide-3 source bar as the cited upstream object
- peel into one compact blue hero block labeled `History reconstruction`
- keep the three reasons inside one grouped support card
- preserve a lot of white space so the slide still feels sparse

### Hybrid File / Archive Metaphor

- use a physical history-object photo as the hero
- keep the support block and bar relationship from the brief
- rejected for canonical pass because it adds taste risk and more visual noise
  than the slide needs

### Literal Pie / Clock Wedge

- make the wedge metaphor more explicit with a symbolic object
- rejected because it starts to feel like a new metaphor instead of a direct
  continuation of slide `3`

## Why the selected direction is right

- it preserves the strongest continuity with slide `3`
- it keeps the slide to one beat instead of reopening the old merged `4/5`
  structure
- it makes the wedge argument fast without adding tactile clutter or mechanism
  detail

## Design rules for v1

- title stays large and direct with no subtitle
- source bar stays visibly quoted, not treated as the hero
- hero stays compact and singular, not a billboard or full lower panel
- support reasons stay grouped as one support cluster
- connector stays one broad editorial arc only

## Selected Direction

### Quoted Bar + Extracted Wedge Card

- promote the sparse native interpretation
- keep the slide-3 bar literal
- make the hero and reasons carry the figure
- keep all slide-4 semantics out of the pass
