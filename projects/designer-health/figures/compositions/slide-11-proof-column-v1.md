## Direction

### Proof Column

- make the slide read top to bottom as one investor argument
- stack the three qualifying conditions first
- land the profile statement in the middle as the thesis
- place the proof panel below as the operational consequence
- keep example settings in a small quiet footer group

## Why this is promising

- strongest match to Claude's critique
- avoids specialty-selection framing
- makes the argument feel memo-like and disciplined

## Design rules

- no market map
- no satellites that imply a specialty taxonomy
- no UI card grid
- conditions must read before examples
- example settings must stay equal-weight and footer-level
- proof panel should read as a ledger, not a table-heavy box
