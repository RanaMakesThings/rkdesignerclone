## Direction

### Power of One Hero Card

- use one oversized hero card as the main economic claim
- hero headline should make the conversion unmistakable:
  - `+1 visit / provider / day = $11,000 / year`
- assumptions should live as small pills or chips
- `~$917 / month break-even spend / provider` should live as a distinct badge
- conservative / base / upside should sit below as small muted cards or chips

## Why this is promising

- more punchy and less arithmetic-looking than the equation version
- keeps the slide investor-facing and premium
- retains the current family while tightening hierarchy

## Design rules

- one dominant hero card
- no dashboard chrome
- scenario row stays clearly secondary
- assumptions must stay visible but quiet
- avoid making the hero feel like a SaaS pricing card
