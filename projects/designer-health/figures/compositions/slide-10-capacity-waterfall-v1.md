## Direction

### Capacity-to-Cash Waterfall

- show the translation from recovered time into contribution value
- use a simplified stepped or waterfall logic:
  - recovered time
  - `+1 visit / provider / day`
  - annualized value
  - contribution margin result
- keep `~$917 / month break-even spend / provider` as the practical badge
- any sensitivity view must be tiny and secondary

## Why this is promising

- makes the operational-to-economic transformation explicit
- more visual than a plain card stack

## Design rules

- the flow must stay simple
- do not let the diagram become finance software
- one dominant final result
- no dense labels or gridlines
