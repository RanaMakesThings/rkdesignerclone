## Figure Job

- Make the Vox workflow feel concrete, trustworthy, and calm before the deck
  moves into the clinician-facing artifact.

## Direction 1: Even Workflow Strip

- visual protagonist:
  - one four-stage horizontal strip with evenly weighted rounded stages
- secondary elements:
  - subtle connectors
  - small trust tags under the later stages
- figure family:
  - `workflow_strip`
- overall layout:
  - title and subtitle sit in normal slide chrome
  - the strip occupies the middle band at roughly 72% to 76% width
- what is big:
  - stage labels
  - the `Structure` stage
- what stays quiet:
  - connectors
  - support text
  - trust tags
- why it works:
  - it matches the clean-pack instruction most directly
  - it reads immediately without looking like process software
- risk:
  - it could become too generic if the internal micro-structure is too sparse

## Direction 2: Handoff Artifact Between Structure and Verify

- visual protagonist:
  - same four-stage strip, but with a small floating brief card between stages 3
    and 4
- why it is tempting:
  - it makes the handoff explicit
- why it is not the first pass:
  - it risks becoming busier and more product-like than the clean-pack asks for

## Direction 3: Split Workflow With Lower Trust Rail

- visual protagonist:
  - top stage strip with a second lower rail for provenance and verification
- why it is tempting:
  - it makes trust explicit
- why it is rejected:
  - it drifts toward a process diagram and over-explains the slide

## Selected Direction

### Even Workflow Strip

- keep the strip itself simple
- let the `Structure` stage carry the trust and artifact texture
- let `Verify` carry the final clinician-use cue
- do not float a separate artifact outside the stage cards in the first pass
