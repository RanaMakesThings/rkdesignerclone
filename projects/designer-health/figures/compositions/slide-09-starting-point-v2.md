## Figure Job

- show that current AI support exists around the visit
- make Vox explicit as the center starting-state layer
- keep the slide calm, strategic, and obvious at a glance

## Direction 1: Journey Strip + Vox Brief

- visual protagonist:
  - one horizontal three-zone journey strip
- center object:
  - blue `Vox-prepared brief` card inside the middle zone
- why it works:
  - most direct translation of the claim
- risk:
  - can feel too diagrammatic if the strip gets too literal

## Direction 2: Encounter Threshold + Vox Brief

- visual protagonist:
  - a central threshold marker where the visit starts
- center object:
  - blue `Vox-prepared brief` card sitting on that threshold
- why it works:
  - strongest way to say Vox changes the starting state
- risk:
  - can become too symbolic if the outer support context is too light

## Direction 3: Hallway Context + Vox Brief

- visual protagonist:
  - quiet clinic hallway or reception corridor
- center object:
  - overlaid blue `Vox-prepared brief` card at the start-of-visit point
- why it works:
  - gives clinical context without arguing
- risk:
  - the photo can compete with the center object

## Direction 4: Clipboard Context + Vox Brief

- visual protagonist:
  - clinician hands and a clipboard or paper surface
- center object:
  - overlaid blue `Vox-prepared brief` card aligned to the paper
- why it works:
  - makes the structured starting object feel tangible
- risk:
  - can drift toward a product/mockup feeling if the card gets too literal

## Cross-direction rules

- outer support zones stay gray and quiet
- center `Vox-prepared brief` is the only strong blue object
- no anti-scribe rhetoric
- no generic empty gap with no clear Vox object
- no fake software UI chrome
