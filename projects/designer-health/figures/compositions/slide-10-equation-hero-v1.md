## Direction

### Single Equation Hero

- make the economics feel causally proven, not just asserted
- use one dominant hero object built around:
  - `+1 visit / provider / day`
  - `x 220 clinic days`
  - `x $125 / visit`
  - `x 40% contribution margin`
  - `= $11,000 / year`
- land `~$917 / month break-even spend / provider` as a quieter punchline
- keep conservative / base / upside in a tiny subordinate strip only

## Why this is promising

- it makes it obvious that one added visit can create this much annual value
- it gives the investor the proof in one glance
- it avoids the blank-card feeling of the legacy comp

## Design rules

- one dominant economics equation object
- no spreadsheet grid
- no equal-weight scenario cards
- the range check must stay visually secondary
- the math chain should feel editorial, not academic
