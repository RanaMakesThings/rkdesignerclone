## Direction A: Direct Territory Argument

- strongest read:
  the words lead and the images confirm
- why it works:
  it makes the market claim clearer than the prior image-led rounds
- risk:
  if the images shrink too much, the slide can become text-heavy

## Direction B: Washed Evidence Plates

- strongest read:
  the images still matter, but they sit behind the claims with a soft wash
- why it works:
  it keeps some visual richness while preserving claim-first hierarchy
- risk:
  if the wash is too strong, the evidence can feel generic
