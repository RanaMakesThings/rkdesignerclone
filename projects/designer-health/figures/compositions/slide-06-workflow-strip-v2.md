## Loop Gate Outcome

The first native pass did not fully satisfy the brief.

The key miss was structural, not polish-level:

- the brief asked for one credible handoff artifact between `Structure` and
  `Verify`
- the first pass made the strip calm and readable, but the handoff was still
  implicit inside the `Structure` card
- that means the loop gate should reopen

## Revision Direction

### Even Workflow Strip With Shared Handoff Brief

- keep the same four-stage strip
- keep `Structure` as the visual star
- add one small shared brief object between `Structure` and `Verify`
- make the shared object feel like a handoff, not a second hero

## Why this is the right revision

- it resolves the missing relationship without changing the slide thesis
- it stays inside the clean-pack family
- it keeps the workflow calm and premium
- it avoids the rejected lower-rail and systems-diagram options

## Design Rules for v2

- the handoff object should sit on the connector between `Structure` and
  `Verify`
- it should echo the `Structured brief` content already inside `Structure`
- it should be smaller than the stage cards and clearly subordinate
- it should make the `Verify` stage read as acting on the same brief
- it should not introduce fake product chrome or extra workflow rails

## Selected Direction

### Even Workflow Strip + Explicit Handoff

- promote the strip concept from v1
- add one reusable connector-level handoff artifact
- sharpen `Verify` so it clearly reads as review and use of the handed-off brief
