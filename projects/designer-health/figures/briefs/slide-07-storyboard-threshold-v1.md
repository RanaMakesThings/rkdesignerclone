# Slide 7 Figure Brief

Header:

- `Vox prepares the visit before it starts.`

Subheader:

- `Vox interviews, structures, and hands off a brief the clinician can verify`
  `and use.`

Scope:

- Design the full slide figure area for slide 7.
- Keep the existing slide thesis, but shift the composition from an equal-step
  process strip to a left-to-right storyboard with explicit temporal rhythm.

Must-keep logic:

- one shared horizontal flow with four unequal beats:
  - `Invite sent`
  - `Adaptive interview`
  - `Brief prepared`
  - `Clinician starts prepared`
- beats 1 to 3 are grouped under `Before visit`
- a vertical threshold marker appears before beat 4 with the label
  `Visit begins`
- beat 3 is the visual hero and larger than the others
- beat 4 is the payoff and should sit closer to beat 3 than beat 1 sits to
  beat 2

Exact beat copy:

- `Invite sent`
  - `Patient receives secure pre-visit link.`
- `Adaptive interview`
  - `Vox asks follow-up questions and captures the story.`
- `Brief prepared`
  - `Patient story becomes a clinician-ready handoff.`
- `Clinician starts prepared`
  - `Sets up a more efficient visit.`

Desired effect:

- read in one left-to-right pass
- feel temporal and product-specific rather than generic-process
- make the handoff into the visit spatially obvious
- keep the slide calm, premium, and editorial

Helpful directions:

- use one thin bracket or hairline band above beats 1 to 3 labeled
  `Before visit`
- use hairline connectors, tiny arrowheads, or motion ticks rather than big
  arrows
- beat 1 should resolve almost instantly:
  phone plus secure-link cue
- beat 2 should make the product feel active:
  phone or patient-with-phone, short exchange, one follow-up
- beat 3 should show compression from story fragments into a structured brief,
  but not duplicate slide 8's full artifact
- beat 4 should show a calm clinician review moment with only a glimpse of the
  packet

Visual rules:

- white background
- grayscale base
- one restrained accent color
- crisp rounded forms
- subtle shadows
- controlled overlays
- readable text rendered directly in the slide, not baked into noisy imagery
- accent should cluster around beat 3, with lighter support in beats 2 and 4

Avoid:

- 2x2 grids
- four equal boxes
- giant arrows or chevrons
- thick flowchart styling
- photoreal stock scenes
- dashboard or enterprise workflow chrome
- making beat 1 visually as important as beats 2 to 4
