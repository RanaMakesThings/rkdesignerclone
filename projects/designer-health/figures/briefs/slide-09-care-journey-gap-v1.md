# Slide 9 Figure Brief

Header:

- `The visit still starts from zero.`

Subheader:

- `Clinicians have shown they will adopt assistive tools. The open problem is`
  `starting the visit with better structure.`

Scope:

- Design the figure / graphic area for the slide.
- The figure may include the slide title and subtitle in the native spec, but
  the visual job is still the care-journey gap object.

Must-keep logic:

- one horizontal care journey from `Before visit` to `During / after`
- make `Start of visit` the unresolved hero zone
- show nearby workflow support as credible context, not as straw-man failures
- supported zones can carry small tags such as:
  - `reminders`
  - `scheduling`
  - `documentation support`
  - `note generation`
  - `clerical relief`
- open zone should imply:
  - `structured starting point`
  - `pre-decision signal`
  - `recovered decision time`

Desired effect:

- make the category gap legible in under 3 seconds
- feel strategic and crisp without becoming a consulting slide
- make it obvious that the missing support sits exactly where the visit begins

Helpful directions:

- one clean rounded journey strip
- three zones:
  `Before visit`, `Start of visit`, `During / after`
- quiet filled zones on the outside
- outlined or accent-highlighted open zone in the middle
- small tags in supported zones, sparse unmet-need cues in the open zone

Avoid:

- competitor matrices
- two-column compare/contrast as the default
- anti-scribe rhetoric
- heavy explanatory captions
- too many tags
- fake product UI or dashboard panels
