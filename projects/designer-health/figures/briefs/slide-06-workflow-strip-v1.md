# Slide 6 Figure Brief

Header:

- `Vox prepares the visit before it starts.`

Subheader:

- `Vox interviews, structures, and hands off a brief the clinician can`
  `verify and use.`

Scope:

- Design the figure / graphic area for the slide.
- The figure may include the slide title and subtitle in the native spec, but
  the visual job is still the workflow object.

Must-keep logic:

- one calm four-step flow:
  `Invite → Interview → Structure → Verify`
- equal-weight stage verbs
- one credible handoff artifact between `Structure` and `Verify`
- small trust cues attached to later stages:
  - `provenance`
  - `explicit unknowns`
  - `verification queue`

Desired effect:

- make the workflow feel operationally credible in two seconds
- show that the work starts before the visit
- keep it restrained, premium, and non-technical

Helpful directions:

- rounded stage strip
- subtle connectors
- accent emphasis on `Structure`
- micro-structure inside the later stages, not fake UI

Avoid:

- swimlanes
- enterprise process-diagram styling
- dense captions
- thick arrows
- fake software chrome
- decorative gradients or infographic clutter
