# Slide 11 Figure Brief

Header:

- `Start where throughput proof is fastest.`

Subheader:

- `The best first market is any setting with visible visit pressure, simple`
  `buying, and low deployment friction.`

Scope:

- Design the figure / graphic area for the slide.
- The figure may include the slide title and subtitle in the native spec, but
  the visual job is the beachhead definition object, not text chrome.

Must-keep logic:

- the beachhead is a profile, not a specialty
- one dominant beachhead profile object:
  - `High-throughput groups with simple deployment`
  - optional small support line:
    `A profile, not a specialty`
- three qualifying conditions:
  - `visible visit pressure`
  - `simple buying`
  - `low deployment friction`
- supporting example settings stay secondary:
  - `Primary care`
  - `GI / gastroenterology`
  - `Orthopedics`
  - `Urgent care`
  - `Multi-site specialty groups`
- one compact proof panel:
  - title:
    `What the first pilot must prove`
  - rows:
    - `patient completion before visit`
    - `clinician verification time`
    - `re-asking reduction in the room`
    - `visit throughput or schedule lift`

Desired effect:

- make the first-market choice feel operational and disciplined
- make it obvious this is about proof speed, not market size
- preserve the cross-specialty point without becoming abstract
- feel premium, calm, and investor-facing

Helpful directions:

- one blue hero profile object in the center or left-center
- example settings as quiet orbiting or row-based context
- one compact scorecard on the right
- strong hierarchy:
  profile first, qualifying conditions second, proof panel third

Avoid:

- funnels
- filters
- consulting scorecards
- TAM / market map visual language
- dense scoring matrices
- making one specialty feel like the whole thesis
