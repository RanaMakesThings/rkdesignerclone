# Slide 10 Figure Brief

Header:

- `Recovered visit time creates real capacity.`

Subheader:

- `When recovered minutes become completed visits, the impact is meaningful.`

Scope:

- Design the figure / graphic area for the slide.
- The figure may include the slide title and subtitle in the native spec, but
  the visual job is the economics object, not the text chrome.

Must-keep logic:

- one dominant base-case economics block
- hero anchors:
  - `+1 visit / provider / day`
  - `$11,000 / year`
  - `~$917 / month break-even spend / provider`
- three smaller range-check scenario tiles:
  - conservative
  - base
  - upside
- assumptions remain visible but quiet:
  - `220 clinic days`
  - `$125 / visit`
  - `40% contribution margin`
- one short bottom-line interpretation:
  `Recovered time matters when it becomes completed visits.`

Desired effect:

- make the economics intuitive in two seconds
- feel like operating leverage, not a spreadsheet
- show a credible base case with enough range to feel real
- keep the slide premium and investor-facing

Helpful directions:

- large left hero block with one dominant annual number
- right-side scenario stack that is clearly secondary
- compressed assumptions line or chip row
- a calm editorial bottom line rather than dense explanation

Avoid:

- tables
- spreadsheet styling
- dashboard chrome
- too many formulas on the page
- over-explaining the math
- making the scenario tiles visually equal to the hero
